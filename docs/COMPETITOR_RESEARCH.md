## Market Overview

### Industry: International Student Recruitment (EdTech)

The international student recruitment market is a $10B+ industry globally. India is the world's second-largest source of international students (after China), sending 1.3M+ students abroad annually as of 2024.

**Key market facts:**
- India sends ~750,000 students to USA, UK, Canada, Australia annually
- Average spend per student: ₹30–80 lakhs (tuition + living)
- Consultancy market in India: ~15,000 registered agents
- Top destinations for Indian students: USA, UK, Canada, Australia, Germany
- Growing destinations: France, Ireland, Cyprus, Estonia, Netherlands

### The Global Avenues' position

- **Niche:** B2B (university representation) + B2C (student counselling)
- **Geography:** South Asia, Middle East, Africa (SAMEA region)
- **Differentiator:** ICEF certified, exclusive MOU partnerships, 12+ years experience
- **Scale:** Mid-size (not a startup, not a giant like IDP)

## Competitor Analysis

### 1. MSM Unify (msmunify.com)

**Overview:** One of India's largest study abroad platforms. Part of MSM Group.

**Strengths:**
- Massive university network (750+ universities)
- Strong brand recognition in India
- Good mobile app
- Comprehensive visa tracking

**Weaknesses:**
- UI feels corporate and cold — not warm or aspirational
- Overwhelming amount of information — poor information hierarchy
- Slow page loads
- Generic design — could be any EdTech company
- No 3D or interactive elements
- Agent portal is clunky

**Design observations:**
- Color: Blue/navy primary
- Typography: Generic sans-serif
- Layout: Dense, information-heavy
- Animations: Minimal

**What we do better:**
- Warmer, more aspirational design
- Interactive 3D globe
- Better information hierarchy
- Faster, more modern tech stack
- More engaging animations

## Design Trend Analysis

### What top EdTech portals are doing in 2025–2026

1. **Dark sections for premium feel** — Used by Notion, Linear, Vercel. We use dark sections for the globe and exclusive partners.

2. **Glassmorphism** — Frosted glass effects on cards and overlays. We use `.glass` utility class.

3. **3D elements** — Spline, Three.js, Globe.GL for interactive 3D. We use Globe.GL.

4. **Gradient text** — Colorful gradient text for headlines. We use `.text-gradient-orange`.

5. **Micro-animations** — Subtle hover effects, scroll reveals. We use Framer Motion throughout.

6. **Warm color palettes** — Moving away from cold blue/grey. We use orange/cream.

7. **Large typography** — 72–96px hero headlines. We use 5–7rem.

8. **Floating notification badges** — Social proof elements. We use in hero section.

9. **Dot/grid patterns** — Subtle background textures. We use `.dot-grid` and `.line-grid`.

10. **Parallax scrolling** — Depth on scroll. We use in hero section.

## Our Unique Differentiators

### 1. Interactive 3D Globe
No competitor has a real interactive 3D globe with arc lines from India to destinations. This is our showstopper feature.

### 2. Exclusive Partner Tier System
We clearly differentiate between Exclusive (MOU) partners and general partners with visual badges. No competitor does this as clearly.

### 3. Warm Indian-Friendly Design
While all competitors use cold blue/corporate design, we use warm orange/cream that resonates with Indian students and their families.

### 4. Combined B2B + B2C
We serve both universities (B2B representation) and students (B2C counselling) in one platform. Most competitors focus on one or the other.

### 5. Real Interactive Tools
Our cost calculator, comparison lab, document butler, and daily quiz are genuinely interactive — not just marketing copy.

### 6. SAMEA Focus
We specifically target South Asia, Middle East, and Africa — a region underserved by Western-focused platforms like ApplyBoard.
